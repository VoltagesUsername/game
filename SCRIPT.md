# The Mongol Empire — 3-minute voiceover script

Record one file per scene and save it in `public/` as `scene1.m4a` … `scene7.m4a`.
Target lengths are shown for each scene; speaking at a calm pace (~2.5 words per
second) fits comfortably. After recording, the animations get re-timed to your voice.

Total: **~3:00**

---

## Scene 1 — Hook & expansion · 22 s
*When were they around?*

> In less than a century, a few nomadic tribes from the Asian grasslands built an empire five times larger than Rome's.
> Between 1206 and 1368, it covered about 23 million square kilometers — roughly a sixth of all the land on Earth.
> How did they do it?

## Scene 2 — Rise to power · 30 s
*What factors led to their establishment?*

> Before 1206, the Mongols were rival tribes locked in blood feuds. Then a leader named Temüjin united them and took the title Genghis Khan.
> He promoted soldiers for skill and loyalty, not royal blood.
> With fast horses, powerful composite bows, and siege weapons built by conquered engineers, his army became unstoppable.

## Scene 3 — Where they were based · 25 s
*Where were they based?*

> From their capital, Karakorum, on the steppes of Mongolia, the empire spread in every direction —
> from the Sea of Japan to Eastern Europe, across China, Russia, Persia, and the Middle East.
> Ruling it took more than an army.

## Scene 4 — Achievements · 30 s
*What were some of their achievements?*

> The Mongols created the Pax Mongolica — the Mongol Peace. They made the Silk Road safe for trade, technology, and ideas.
> The Yam, a relay of horse stations, carried messages thousands of kilometers in days.
> One legal code, the Yassa, applied across the empire, and many religions were tolerated.

## Scene 5 — Daily life & women · 30 s
*What was it like to live there? What was life like for women?*

> Most Mongols lived on the move, herding sheep and horses and packing their gers onto carts in a few hours. They lived on meat and dairy, like airag — fermented mare's milk.
> Women had far more freedom than in medieval Europe or China: they owned property, traded livestock, and ran the camps.
> Royal women, the Khatuns, even ruled as regents.

## Scene 6 — Decline & fall · 30 s
*Why did they come to an end?*

> But the empire had no clear rule for who would be the next Khan. After 1260 it split into four khanates, and Mongol rulers adopted local religions and languages.
> In the 1340s the Black Death devastated populations and trade, and in 1368 the Red Turban Rebellion drove the Mongols out of China.

## Scene 7 — Outro · 13 s

> From scattered tribes to the largest connected land empire in history, the Mongols reshaped trade, politics, and the world we know today.

---

### Fact notes (already fixed in this version)
- The empire covered about **16%** of Earth's land ("about a sixth"), not 20%.
- The Mongols **raided** Hungary in 1241 but never held it; the map shows a raid arrow.
- The Red Turban Rebellion **began in 1351**; it drove the Mongols out of China in **1368**.
- The name "Yuan Dynasty" dates from **1271**.
